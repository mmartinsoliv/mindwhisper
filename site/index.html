Speak anywhere. It types for you.

Mindwhisper is a dictation app for macOS and Windows. Press a shortcut in any app, speak Portuguese (Brazil) or English, and tidy text lands in the field you were typing in.

How it works

  1. 1

    Press the shortcut

    Default: Ctrl or Cmd + Shift + Space, in any app.

  2. 2

    Speak

    A small overlay shows it is listening.

  3. 3

    Press it again

    The text is cleaned up and pasted into the field you were in.

What it does

Works in any app

Pastes into whatever field has focus.

Portuguese and English

Português do Brasil, English, or automatic.

Optional AI cleanup

Fix punctuation, drop filler words, pick a style: minimal, professional, casual or concise. Or keep your exact words.

Personal dictionary

Names and terms it should get right.

History you control

Kept on your computer only, with a retention period, and you can turn it off.

Stays out of the way

Lives in the tray. Closing the window keeps dictation working.

Privacy

  • While you dictate, audio is sent to the Mindwhisper server and on to a speech-to-text provider. Nothing is sent when you are not dictating.
  • Audio is never written to disk.
  • The recognized text goes to an AI provider for cleanup, unless you turn cleanup off or choose to keep your words.
  • History and your dictionary stay on your computer, and “Delete all my data” removes them.
  • Provider keys live only on the server, never in the app.

    Install help

    macOS

    1. Open the .dmg and drag Mindwhisper to Applications.
    2. The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again (or System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway).
    3. Allow Microphone when asked, and turn on Accessibility for Mindwhisper in System Settings, Privacy & Security, so it can paste.

    macOS may ask for these permissions again after an update.

    Windows

    1. Run the installer.
    2. If “Windows protected your PC” appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
    3. Allow microphone access for desktop apps in Settings, Privacy, Microphone.

    These warnings appear because the early builds are not code-signed yet.

    FAQ

    Does it work offline?

    No. Speech recognition runs on the server, so you need an internet connection.

    Which languages?

    Português do Brasil and English, with automatic detection.

    Is my history uploaded?

    No. It stays on your computer.

    Why does it need Accessibility on a Mac?

    To paste text into other apps. Without it the text stays on the clipboard.
